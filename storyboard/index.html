Physics and movement

  • Why cannon-es advances with a fixed 1/120-second physics step, separately from rendering.
  • Why frame-time caps and a substep limit keep catch-up work bounded.
  • How grounded movement follows supporting surfaces while airborne movement retains vertical velocity.
  • Why a 120 Hz physics step does not mean the game always renders at 120 FPS.

Useful example: walking up a slope, jumping, landing and meeting a solid obstacle.

Code reference: scenePhysics.ts, fixed-step loop.

  • How the cursor angle selects a segment and the centre dead zone keeps the current weapon.

Useful example: equip a weapon, cancel a selection and return to Unarmed.
