Voice-over & AI character voices

AI-generated dialogue for Prime, Brondon, Branden, Brendan, Donus, Caleb, Husain, André, Sibusiso and Sohrab. Sudoers 5 group dialogue combines five separate generated performances.

Synthetic fictional performances generated with Kyutai Pocket TTS (model weights: CC BY 4.0), through the free Pocket TTS demo hosted by D3vShoaib.
Prime's original voice designed with Qwen3-TTS (Apache-2.0).
Other cast references: CSTR VCTK Corpus 0.92 by Junichi Yamagishi, Christophe Veaux and Kirsten MacDonald, via Kyutai's voice catalogue under CC BY 4.0; enhanced by ai-coustics. Generated dialogue, pitch/EQ processing and five-voice group mixes are modifications. No provider or dataset speaker endorsement is implied.
